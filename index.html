I'm Tushant, a computer science student exploring the space between reliable systems and expressive interfaces. My projects range from payment reconciliation and monitoring tools to APIs and small Python experiments.

I care about what happens after the happy path: duplicate events, invalid inputs, unavailable services, and states that should never pretend to be something they aren't.

Right now, I'm sharpening my foundations in data structures, algorithms, and machine learning—and learning by building things I can explain.
